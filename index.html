About the proposed rules

University students aged 18–24 upload evidence for a proposed SheerID check every year. Re-verification runs 1 September–31 October of the following calendar year, including for November sign-ups. Provider coverage, ISIC acceptance and returned fields require confirmation; this prototype uses university IDs only.

Benefits remain active through the October deadline. Successful renewal advances the window to next year. Expiry pauses student benefits but does not prove graduation or start a reward. Confirming graduation starts six calendar months of offers immediately.

The 10% student discount, student and graduation 5% extra Asia Miles, one-year family lock and lounge passes are proposed terms, not existing Cathay entitlements. Progress remains displayed, not a guaranteed extension of Status Point validity. Age 18–24 follows the Student Pass slide; the separate 18–25 caption needs alignment.

Family bonuses are capped at 8%; no passenger-to-rate formula or stacking rule is assumed and no bonus miles are credited here. Three qualifying completed journeys unlock one family pass, separate from the graduation pass. Pass coverage, expiry, reset periods and lounge admission require approval. Guardian transfers debit the child and credit the guardian only, not the shared pool.

Prototype only: no support request was sent. A live service needs a reviewed route for mistaken joins, safeguarding and exceptional lock overrides.
About this competition prototype

Independent concept, not an official Cathay app. All verification, family links, QR scans, codes, transfers, alerts and lounge passes are simulated. No live SheerID integration or actual entitlements. University coverage and proposed offers require confirmation.

The saved student ID and membership details are fictional. File selection shows a filename only; contents are not read, extracted, stored or sent. Use fictional documents, not real personal IDs. State resets on reload.

Minor detection uses fictional membership birth dates and an under-18 threshold: choose Sam to see the automatic alert; Percy and Daniel follow adult acceptance. Production must use verified birth dates and approved age rules.

Production needs secure QR and code validation, identity and relationship checks, guardian consent and reviewed safety exceptions. The university list does not imply SheerID coverage.
